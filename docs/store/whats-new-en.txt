A connection form made for S3. In the connections window (Ctrl+F) an S3 connection now has its own fields: pick the provider (Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, Wasabi, or MinIO and other servers of your own), then the region or the service address, the Access Key ID with the Secret Access Key, and the bucket with a folder to start in. Amazon builds the address from the region; a pasted URL is split into host, port and HTTP; the secret key can be typed in the form and is kept in the Windows Credential Manager. Connections saved earlier open as before.
